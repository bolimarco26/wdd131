Website Planning Document

Site Name

RideNow Bolivia

RideNow Bolivia was selected because it communicates speed, convenience, and transportation services. The name is easy to remember and reflects the goal of creating a modern ride-booking platform that can begin as a small local business and grow into a larger transportation service.

Site Purpose

RideNow Bolivia is a transportation reservation website that allows customers to request a private ride quickly and easily. Users will be able to complete an online reservation form, review available services, and contact the driver. The website is designed as the first Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for a future transportation startup.

Target Audience

  • People who need safe transportation within the city.
  • Students.
  • Families.
  • Workers who need scheduled transportation.
  • Visitors looking for a reliable transportation service.

Scenarios

  1. How can I request a ride from my location to my destination?
  2. How much could my trip cost before making a reservation?
